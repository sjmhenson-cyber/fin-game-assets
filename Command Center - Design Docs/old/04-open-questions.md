# Open Questions

Unresolved decisions. Move an item into the GDD and delete it from here
once it's decided.

1. **Stamina vs. Vault** — leisure spending doesn't reduce the Vault, so
   spent money never actually leaves the fightable pool. Is leisure spend
   carved out of Deposit before it hits the Vault, or is it genuinely
   separate money?
2. **What is a "turn"?** — Nothing in the game currently has a turn
   structure. Candidates: a real payday, a calendar week, a login session.
   This gates combat pacing, income-to-damage mapping, and interest timing.
3. **"No stores" vs. Ēkehya upgrades** — Ēkehya's Upgrade path is
   functionally a merchant (spend gold, get a permanent item) even though
   it isn't framed as a shop. Worth confirming this framing is acceptable
   against the "no stores" constraint, or adjusting it.
4. **Errand/courier system** — small interpersonal debt (Venmo-type) has a
   verb (deliver) but no actual mechanic designed yet.
5. **Closure moment for a slain boss** — currently just a greyed-out card.
   Given how central "slaying real debt" is, does it need a trophy room,
   permanent record, or other lasting payoff?
6. **Ledger-edit detection** — needs an actual diff/reconciliation design,
   not just a flavor message, so the Log doesn't develop silent gaps when
   the Sheet is hand-edited.
