1. Game Design Document (GDD) & Architecture Guide
Core Loop
Work (Real Life): Players earn real-life paychecks through their respective businesses.

Audit & Collect: On payday, the game alerts players to audit and collect their income into their separate Vaults.

Fight (Pay Debts): Players navigate the map and encounter "Keter-class" anomaly bosses (debts). Players use "Channel Strikes" to input real-world debt payments, permanently lowering the boss's Max HP.

Explore & Craft: Players use weekly Stamina (leisure budget) to fight random encounters for ingredient drops.

Upgrade: Players spend Vault Gold at the Swedish Keep to expand the home base or maintain appliances (like the Blueprint Drill-Driver) for buffs.

Player Characters
Sarah (The Culinary Arcanist): Uses baking/alchemy for support and combat.

Weapon: Blueprint Drill-Driver (upgradable).

Moves: Obsidian Strike, Aegis of the Hearth, Flash Focus, Macaron Mend.

Taliyah (The Dimensional Weaver): Uses sci-fi anomalies and radiant energy.

Weapon: Tachyon Gauntlet.

Moves: Luminous Pierce, Isotope Ward, Spirit Bash, Stasis Module.

Hunter (The Battle-Poodle): AI companion.

Moves: High-Pitch Bark (Stun), Slow Blink (Defense Drop).

Mechanic: Fed homemade dog treats (crafted from monster meat drops) to trigger "Rescue" (Party-wide heal).

Map Zones & Gated Progression
The Plains (Hub): Contains the shared home, Sarah's Bakery (The Hearth), Taliyah's Animal Sanctuary, and the Bonfire checkpoint.

The Bridge (Tutorial Gate): Guarded by the Vanguard of Venn-Mhor. Must be defeated (Venmo balance hits zero) to access the Whispering Woods.

The Keep of Ēkehya: The Swedish merchant fortress run by tall, faceless builders. Receives gold in exchange for permanent home upgrades.

Sapphire Lake: Mid-game zone guarded by the Sapphire Gargoyle (Chase Reserve).

The Unification Event
Once all Keter-class anomaly bosses are defeated, the individual Vaults permanently fuse into a single Family Treasury, unlocking endgame town-building mechanics and shared progression.




Command Center RPG: Technical & Mechanics Specification
1. The Game Loop & State Machine
The game operates on a continuous, live-service loop tied to real-world time and data. The engine relies on a state machine that transitions between three primary views:

Overworld State (The Hub): The default isometric/top-down view where players navigate The Plains, interact with NPCs, forage, and trigger shop menus.

Combat State: Triggered by interacting with a Boss (Anomaly) or wandering into tall grass (Random Encounters). The UI locks into a turn-based battle screen.

Audit State: Triggered by a real-world calendar event (Payday). Halts overworld exploration until the player acknowledges or edits their incoming Vault deposit.

2. The Economy & Dual Vault System
The game features a two-phase economic engine powered by the Google Sheet (Finances tab).

Phase 1: Dual Economy (Early/Mid Game)
Sarah’s Vault: Funded by "The Hearth" (Bakery).

Taliyah’s Vault: Funded by the "Animal Sanctuary."

The Payday Audit: On scheduled real-world paydays, an alert icon (!) spawns over both businesses.

Logic: Player taps the building -> UI presents the Expected Yield (e.g., $2,000) -> Player taps [Collect] (deposits exactly $2k) OR [Audit] (opens a keypad to input the exact real-world paycheck).

Isolated Spending: Sarah can only spend her Vault gold for her upgrades; Taliyah spends hers. However, they can both contribute gold to the same boss.

Phase 2: Unification (Endgame)
The Trigger: When the game detects that the final Keter-class boss HP equals 0, the vaults_merged boolean in the JSON configuration flips to true.

The Result: The UI permanently merges the two Vaults into a "Family Treasury." All future business income pools into one massive shared wallet for endgame town-building.

3. Debt Anomalies & Channel Strikes
Monsters blocking the map (e.g., Vanguard of Venn-Mhor, Sapphire Gargoyle) represent real-world debts.

Health Points (HP): Boss Max HP = Total Debt Balance.

The "Channel Strike" Mechanic: Players do not use standard attacks on Bosses.

Logic: Player taps [Channel Strike] -> Inputs the exact real-world dollar amount they just paid toward the debt -> Engine subtracts that amount from the Player's Vault AND the Boss's HP.

Milestone Gating: When Boss HP hits exactly 0, the entity shatters, and the overworld map dynamically updates to remove the roadblock, granting access to the next zone.

4. Turn-Based Combat Logic (Random Encounters)
Unlike Bosses, random encounters (foraging for materials) use traditional RPG logic.

The Stamina System: Fighting costs Stamina (representing real-world leisure energy). Stamina caps at 100 and replenishes by 25 points at the start of every real-world week.

Combat Flow: Speed stat determines turn order.

Co-Op Synergy Multiplier: If Sarah and Taliyah both participate in a combat encounter within the same 24-hour real-world window, a Synergy Buff activates, applying a 1.5x multiplier to all damage dealt.

Character Move Sets & Logic
Sarah (Culinary Arcanist):

Weapon: Blueprint Drill-Driver.

Moves: Flash Focus (Lowers enemy accuracy by 20%), Macaron Mend (Restores 30% HP to one ally).

Taliyah (Dimensional Weaver):

Moves: Spirit Bash (Deals physical damage and boosts speed), Stasis Module (Applies "Stun" status, skipping the enemy's next turn).

Hunter (The AI Battle-Poodle):

Logic: Hunter operates autonomously. At the end of every round, he has a 15% RNG chance to cast Slow Blink (lowers enemy defense) or High-Pitch Bark (interrupts an enemy attack).

The Defeat Penalty
If a player's HP hits 0 during a random encounter, they "Faint."

Logic: No items or gold are lost. The player is teleported back to the Bonfire in The Plains. HP is restored to 100%, but their weekly Stamina pool is penalized by 15 points.

5. Crafting & Hunter's Diet
Players farm materials from random encounters to craft items.

Meat Drops: Defeating organic monsters yields "Mystery Meat," which goes into the Shared Inventory.

Baking Dog Treats: Sarah can combine Meat + Foraged Berries at the Bakery oven to craft "Hunter's Treats."

The Rescue Mechanic: Feeding Hunter a treat during a battle consumes a turn but guarantees he will cast Rescue, a massive party-wide heal (restores 70% HP to all allies).

6. The Keep of Ēkehya (Upgrades & Buffs)
The Swedish Keep is the primary merchant for home improvements.

Appliance Durability: Items like the Bakery Oven degrade slightly over time. Players can spend basic raw materials (Wood/Ore) to repair them, granting a temporary 10% buff to crafted items.

Permanent Upgrades: Players spend Vault Gold at Ēkehya to completely replace an appliance.

Logic: Purchasing the "Modern Oven" permanently swaps the 16x16 sprite in the overworld and applies a permanent base-stat increase to all crafted baked goods.

7. Dynamic Expansion (Adding New Content)
The game is built to run infinitely. The developer must ensure the Google Sheet can hot-reload the game state.

Adding a Boss: If the player takes out a new car loan, they simply add a new row to the Lexicon tab in Excel (e.g., "Steel Chariot") and a new row in the Finances tab. The game engine dynamically reads this and spawns a new Keter-class boss on the map without requiring code deployment.
